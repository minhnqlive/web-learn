A Little Field Guide to Leaves

Three shapes to look for on your next walk.

Start with the outline

Look where the blade meets the stem, then trace the edge with your eyes.

  • Oval

    Look for a broad middle and an outline that narrows toward both ends.

  • Heart-shaped

    Find the notch at the base and the pointed tip at the opposite end.

  • Palmately lobed

    Look for lobes spreading from a common point like fingers from a palm.
